How to read this

  • Relevance (●●●) favours Canadian funders, calls Canadian researchers can lead, microbiome-specific calls, and human microbiome work.
  • Estimated dates are projected from the previous cycle and marked “~”. Entries flagged verify have details not yet confirmed on the funder's page.
  • Amounts are converted to CAD at approximate rates for filtering; “avg.” means a typical award rather than a maximum. Always confirm on the funder's site before applying.
  • Listings are refreshed weekly. Closed calls move to the archive (“Show closed”) because many recur every year.
